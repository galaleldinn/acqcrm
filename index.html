📜 Floor Attendance Rules, KPIs & Leave Policy

Official Standard Operating Procedures for all Acquisition Outbound Callers.

Active Floor Policy
⏱️

1. Shift Hours & Profile Verification

  • •Verified Google Sign-In: Every caller must sign in with their own personal Google account. Your Gmail binds permanently to your roster profile—never share logins or clock in for a teammate.
  • •Target Shift Duration: Full shift target is 8.0 net working hours (excluding break time). All timestamps are recorded in Egypt Time (Africa/Cairo).
  • •Cumulative Daily Tracking: Pausing and resuming your shift accumulates active hours, break minutes, and calls cleanly without resetting.
☕

2. Break Allowance & Live Floor Alerts

  • •Total Daily Break Quota: Standard allowance is 80 minutes per shift (unless customized by Admin). Exceeding quota triggers a 🚨 Break Over flag.
  • •Single Break Cap: Any individual break longer than 30 minutes is flagged as ⏱️ Long Break.
  • •Live Floor Overtime Alert: Any active break exceeding 20 minutes flashes an immediate alert on the Live Floor Monitor. Always click Resume Work upon return.
📞

3. Dialing KPIs & EOD Reporting

  • •Minimum Dialing Benchmark: Maintain at least 25 calls per net working hour. Shifts below this pace are flagged as 📉 Low Calls/Hr.
  • •Mid-Shift Pulse: Log your current calls and leads midway through your shift using the ⚡ Mid-Shift Pulse button.
  • •Mandatory EOD Submission: You cannot complete clock-out without entering your exact Total Calls, Talk Minutes, and Qualified Leads.
🌴

4. Annual Leave Quotas & Request Rules

  • •Annual Leave (15 Days / Year): Planned time off. Submit in advance via the 📅 Leave Calendar for manager approval.
  • •Casual Leave (6 Days / Year): Short-notice personal days, deducted only when approved.
  • •Paid Sick Leave (10 Days / Year): Paid medical days off (requires manager approval).
  • •UPL — Unpaid Leave (Unlimited): Available if paid quotas are exhausted. Unapproved absences are marked Absent / No-Show.
