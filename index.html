📜 Floor Attendance Rules, KPIs & Leave Policy

Official Standard Operating Procedures for all Acquisition Outbound Callers.

Active Floor Policy
⏱️

1. Shift Hours & Profile Verification

  • •Verified Google Sign-In: Every caller must sign in with their own personal Google account. Your Gmail binds permanently to your roster profile—never share logins or clock in for a teammate.
  • •Target Shift Duration: Full shift target is 8.0 net working hours (excluding break time).
  • •Early Clock-Out Gate: Logging out before completing your full shift hours automatically triggers a ⚠️ Short Shift flag and requires selecting an approved reason.
☕

2. Break Allowance & Live Floor Alerts

  • •Total Daily Break Quota: Standard allowance is 80 minutes per shift (unless customized for your schedule by Admin). Exceeding your daily quota triggers a 🚨 Break Over flag.
  • •Single Break Cap: Any individual break longer than 30 minutes is flagged as ⏱️ Long Break.
  • •Live Floor Overtime Alert: Any active break exceeding 20 minutes flashes an immediate overtime alert on the manager's Live Floor Monitor. Always select the accurate break tag (Meal, Prayer, Restroom, or Technical) and click Resume Work immediately upon return.
📞

3. Dialing Pace, Mid-Shift Pulse & EOD Gate

  • •Minimum Dialing Benchmark: Maintain at least 25 calls per net working hour. Shifts below this pace are flagged as 📉 Low Calls/Hr.
  • •Mid-Shift Pulse: Log your current calls and leads midway through your shift using the ⚡ Mid-Shift Pulse button so floor pace stays updated.
  • •Mandatory EOD Submission: You cannot complete clock-out without entering your exact Total Calls, Talk Minutes, and Qualified Leads from your dialer/CRM.
🌴

4. Annual Leave Quotas & Request Rules

  • •Annual Leave (15 Days / Year): Planned time off. Submit in advance via the 📅 Leave Calendar for manager approval.
  • •Casual Leave (6 Days / Year): Short-notice personal days, deducted automatically once approved.
  • •Paid Sick Leave (10 Days / Year): Paid medical days off (requires manager approval).
  • •UPL — Unpaid Leave (Unlimited): Available if paid quotas are exhausted or for extended personal leave. Unapproved absences are marked Absent / No-Show on the Daily Floor Handoff.
